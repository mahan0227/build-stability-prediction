Objective 1 · datasets & parameter-outcome evidence

What the evidence says — literature and corpus, side by side

The review distilled 397 candidate records into 18 auditable primary studies, positioned inside a 37-work bibliography that spans CI/CD foundations, methodology anchors (PRISMA 2020, ROC-AUC caveats) and the field's newest evidence threads. The empirical track then tested whether the review's dataset rules survive contact with real GitHub Actions traces. On the frozen 3,000-run snapshot, process-intensity fields (`duration_sec`, `run_attempt`) carry the significant associations; change-scope fields do not.

Objective 2 · model design (POC)

Baseline models confirm the signal is modest on real CI traces

The frozen Objective 1 snapshot is a standardized logistic regression on the 3,000-run GitHub Actions corpus. Accuracy is high because most runs succeed; failure recall is the metric that matters operationally. Objective 2 is a starter Random Forest POC and was not re-fit on this snapshot.
